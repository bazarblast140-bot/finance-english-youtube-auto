india_states.geojson

Public domain. State outlines are simplified from Natural Earth 1:50m
cultural vectors, admin-1 states and provinces:

    https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

Natural Earth releases that data into the public domain. This file is a
Douglas–Peucker simplification (about 0.045 degrees) for on-screen drawing,
not a survey or legal boundary. Hindi names were added in this repository.
